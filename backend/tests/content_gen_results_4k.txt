## Business Analytics - Week 4: Analytics Techniques and Tools

### 1. MODULE OVERVIEW

**Purpose:** This module aims to deepen students' understanding of foundational analytical techniques used in business, focusing on descriptive and diagnostic analytics. It introduces students to practical tools for data manipulation and statistical analysis, bridging conceptual knowledge with hands-on application.

**Place in course:** This module follows foundational weeks covering data fundamentals and an introduction to business analytics (e.g., Week 1: Introduction to Business Analytics & Data Types; Week 2: Data Collection & Preparation; Week 3: Data Visualization Fundamentals). Students are expected to have a basic understanding of business problems, data types (quantitative vs. qualitative, nominal, ordinal, interval, ratio), data cleaning concepts, and the principles of effective data visualization. This week sets the stage for more advanced predictive and prescriptive analytics in subsequent modules.

**Estimated contact hours:** 3 hours

### 2. MODULE LEARNING OUTCOMES (MLOs)

Upon successful completion of this module, students will be able to:

*   **MLO 4.1:** Differentiate between descriptive and diagnostic analytics techniques and their appropriate application in business contexts.
*   **MLO 4.2:** Apply fundamental statistical measures (e.g., mean, median, mode, standard deviation, correlation) using a specified analytics tool to describe and summarize business datasets.
*   **MLO 4.3:** Interpret analytical outputs to explain patterns, trends, and relationships within business data, informing initial problem diagnosis.

## 1. MODULE OVERVIEW

### Purpose
Week 4 introduces students to the foundational analytics techniques and contemporary tools used in business decision-making. Students move from data fundamentals (Weeks 1–3) to practical application of analytical methods, building competency in selecting appropriate tools and techniques for different business problems.

### Place in Course Sequence
- **Prerequisite knowledge:** Data types, sources, and basic data cleaning (Weeks 1–3)
- **Where it sits:** Mid-point bridge between theory and applied analytics projects (Weeks 5–7)
- **Feeds into:** Advanced analytics and predictive modelling (Weeks 5–6)

### Estimated Contact Hours
- **Synchronous:** 3 hours (lecture + guided practice)
- **Asynchronous:** 2–3 hours (readings, independent practice, reflection)
- **Total:** 5–6 hours student engagement

---

## 2. MODULE LEARNING OUTCOMES (MLOs)

By the end of Week 4, students will be able to:

| MLO | Learning Outcome | Bloom's Level | Measurement |
|-----|------------------|---------------|------------|
| **MLO 1** | **Identify and classify** analytics techniques (descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, prescriptive) appropriate for distinct business problems | *Understand & Apply* | Case analysis; technique-to-problem matching exercise |
| **MLO 2** | **Evaluate and select** suitable analytics tools based on data characteristics, scalability, and organisational context | *Analyze & Evaluate* | Tool comparison matrix; tool selection justification |
| **MLO 3** | **Demonstrate proficiency** using at least two analytics tools (e.g., Excel, Python, Tableau, Power BI) to conduct exploratory data analysis | *Apply* | Hands-on lab with guided practice; reproducible analysis output |
| **MLO 4** | **Synthesize** findings from multiple analytical techniques to support a business recommendation | *Evaluate & Create* | Mini-case report; peer feedback session |

**1. MODULE OVERVIEW**
   Purpose: This module introduces students to various analytics techniques and tools used in business decision-making, building on their understanding of data analysis and statistical concepts.
   Place in course: Week 4 of the Business Analytics course, following weeks that covered data preparation, statistical analysis, and data visualization.
   Estimated contact hours: 3 hours

**2. MODULE LEARNING OUTCOMES (MLOs)**
By the end of this module, students will be able to:
1. Identify and explain the role of different analytics techniques (descriptive, predictive, prescriptive) in solving business problems.
2. Apply appropriate analytics tools (such as Excel, Python libraries, or R) to analyze business data and draw meaningful insights.
3. Evaluate the strengths and limitations of various analytics techniques and tools in different business contexts.

**3. INSTRUCTIONAL SEQUENCE**
| Phase | Time | Activity / Content | Delivery Method | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction / Hook | 15 minutes | Review of previous weeks, introduction to analytics techniques and tools, stating the MLOs | Lecture | Slides 1-3, Whiteboard |
| Core Content Delivery | 45 minutes | Overview of descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive analytics; demonstration of Excel, Python, and R for analytics tasks | Lecture with Demonstration | Slides 4-10, Software tutorials |
| Guided Practice / Activity | 60 minutes | Guided exercise: Students work in pairs to apply analytics tools to a business case study | Guided Practice | Case Study Handout, Access to analytics software |

   Type: In-class problem
   Description: Students will be given a simplified business scenario and asked to choose and apply an appropriate analytics technique to solve the problem.
   MLO Alignment: MLO 1 and MLO 2
   Estimated time: 20 minutes
